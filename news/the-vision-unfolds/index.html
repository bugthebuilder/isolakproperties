THE PINE-VIEW COTTAGE PHASE 1: THE VISION UNFOLDS

Jun 20, 2026

10:33 am

Written by: Temidayo Oginni

Isolak Properties is currently executing a major construction project at The Pine-View Cottage Estate, Phase 1, located in Olooka Village, off Omi Adio, Eruwa Road, Ido, Ibadan. The ongoing development focuses on the erection of the perimeter fence and the construction of the estate gatehouse, marking a significant milestone in transforming the location into a highly secure and modern residential community.

 

This rapid physical transformation is an effort to improve the infrastructure of the estate and encourage plot owners to start construction of their dream home, while also contributing to the socio-economic development of the environment where the estate is located.

Although, development in the estate is a combined effort of the developer and the plot owners, where development fees paid by the customers are strategically channelled into critical boundary and access structures. The company is actively fulfilling its promise to deliver top-tier value, ensuring the estate's security and aesthetic appeal are firmly established early in its lifecycle.

To sustain this developmental momentum and unlock further essential upgrades, Isolak Properties is urgently imploring all clients to stay up to date with  their infrastructure fees. The timely remittance of these fees is vital for the continuity of the current projects and will fund subsequent developments, ensuring the estate evolves into a fully realized world-class neighborhood, with higher ROI (Return on Investment) for all its investors.
